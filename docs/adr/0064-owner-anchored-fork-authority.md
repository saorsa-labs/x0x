## Context

ADR 0059 shipped InviteV4 authentication, owner-countersigned Home invites, the owner head attestation, and local fork evidence. It explicitly deferred the protocol response to this ADR, and the independent triage of #472 (2026-09-05, P1) confirms the residual: authentication alone does not choose the canonical fork.

Three gaps remain on current main `3030de434336d00e4aa855aafa5023abb696c974` (merge of #534; tree `310a06a`, verified 2026-09-06):

1. **No pre-mutation owner mandate.** The head attestation is signed in `stage_join_result` (named_groups.rs:27306) AFTER the authority mutated TreeKEM and persisted; it is enforced only by the adopting joiner's across-gap path (`try_adopt_member_added_across_gap`, named_groups.rs:3309 / owner-axis branch ~3495). The gapless apply path (`validate_apply`, state_commit.rs:928) checks signer-admin-in-roster only, so a `MemberAdded` the admission owner never blessed applies cleanly on every member trusting the same roster.
2. **Alternate-chain authority validation is signer-vs-retained-base.** Adoption validates each link's committer against the reconstructed roster, so (a) a canonically-removed admin still valid at the invite base can drive adoption/eviction, and (b) an admin promoted after the base is wrongly rejected. Canonical branch selection for eviction/rollback has no independent anchor at all.
3. **No persistent route-complete quarantine.** `owner_cert_reverify_required` (groups/mod.rs:330) is `#[serde(skip)]`-transient and ADR-0038-scoped; no data-plane route outside named_groups (public send/cache, secure ratchet/open, history, delegations) consults fork state, and there is no forensic fork snapshot or lifecycle epoch around irreversible ratchet/persist/publish steps.

The stale-base scenario (#468) is unchanged in structure: a joiner at revision N receives an internally consistent N+1 fork from an admin removed at the canonical N+1; every existing check passes; the next canonical commit `PrevHashMismatch`es.

## Decision Drivers

- An invite-derived seat must not be *chosen* by the inviter's chain alone; for OwnerCertified groups only the admission owner can authenticate "this chain is canonical"; for non-owner-axis groups NO automatic canonical anchor is claimed by this ADR (quorum variants rejected — see Option 6 and the r4 removal).
- The fix must roll out through a mixed fleet (v0.40.4 ↔ v0.41.x ↔ mandate-aware) without wedging joins, and must not weaken InviteV4, ADR-0016 chain validation, or ADR-0038 reverify semantics.
- Fail-closed on missing anchors is required for owner-axis groups, but keyless-owner v0.41 admins exist (#472 rollout matrix), so the mandate must be capability-gated, not unconditional.
- Quarantine must be observable, persistent, bounded, and must never silently diverge authorization state from what members can re-derive.

## Considered Options

**Option 1 — Pre-commit owner mandate on `MemberAdded` (per #472 §1).**
Add `owner_mandate: Option<OwnerMandate>` to the `MemberAdded` event: an owner USER-key signature over a preimage whose EVERY member is deterministically known BEFORE the authority mutates TreeKEM/persists — see Decision §1a for the exact derivation, which exists precisely to avoid the mutation circularity of signing "the terminal commit" that the mutation produces. Produced at the pre-mutation point in the MemberJoined handler (the post-mutation head attestation remains as the terminal CAS confirmation), and validated by EVERY enforcing member in the MemberAdded apply path, not only the joiner.
*Rejected as the whole answer, kept as Decision §1:* it closes G1 and anchors the joiner's seat, but says nothing about eviction/rollback fork choice between full members (G2) or data-plane containment (G3).

**Option 2 — Alternate-chain authority validation anchored on the owner mandate (per #472 §2).**
Validate a candidate fork chain from the common ancestor, requiring at each step a committer who was an active admin at that revision's PREDECESSOR roster (walk the retained chain, not the invite base), and require an owner-signed head anchor at the fork point for OwnerCertified groups. Without an independent anchor, name the behaviour "quarantine on authenticated fork evidence" and document the old-key availability attack rather than deciding eviction with a possibly-removed admin's key.
*Accepted as Decision §2 with the quarantine naming:* pure ancestor-walk fixes the promoted-admin rejection but cannot distinguish two valid chains; eviction decisions are therefore NOT automated in this ADR.

**Option 3 — Fully automated fork eviction.**
On authenticated conflicting evidence, evict the losing chain fleet-wide.
*Rejected:* with equal-revision siblings both signed by currently-valid admins, eviction requires an anchor nobody holds at that moment (exactly ADR-0016 Decision 7); automating it converts an availability residual into a split-brain risk. Remains deferred with evidence only.

**Option 4 — Persistent, route-complete quarantine (per #472 §3).**
A centralized operational-state gate keyed by (map id, stable id) — a persisted `fork_quarantined` marker (NOT `#[serde(skip)]`), set only on authenticated first-complete ForkEvidence (already deduplicated by ADR-0059) and cleared only by an owner-anchored canonical commit advancing past the evidence revision — consulted by a defined, enumerated route set with a lifecycle epoch re-checked around irreversible ratchet/persist/cache/publish steps, plus a forensic fork snapshot with `shared_secret` redacted.
*Accepted as Decision §3, but staged:* the full route set (history, delegations, kv cache) is its own review; this ADR commits to the enumerated membership-gated surfaces first.

**Option 5 — Do nothing beyond ADR-0059 observability.**
*Rejected:* explicitly disallowed by the #472 triage ("no fake closure from observability").

**Option 6 — Quorum of members' heads as the anchor (majority-vote head).**
*Rejected:* flat-admin groups have no membership floor guarantee; a 2-member group's "quorum" is the attacker; and it reintroduces sybil-weighting questions ADR-0016 deliberately avoided.

## Decision

1. **Owner mandate (OwnerCertified groups only, capability-gated).** The authority produces an owner USER-key `OwnerMandate` at the pre-mutation point; `MemberAdded` carries it; every member's apply path validates it when the local policy has an owner axis and the mandate is present (absence handling: the capability/grace state machine in Decision §1b). The existing post-mutation head attestation remains as the terminal CAS confirmation and continues to anchor joiner adoption.

   **1a — Mandate preimage (deterministic, pre-mutation, no circularity, over the authority's ACTUAL current roster).** The mandate signs
   `blake3("x0x.owner-mandate.v2\0" ‖ stable_group_id ‖ expected_terminal_revision(LE u64) ‖ parent_state_hash ‖ roster_root_after_add ‖ policy_hash ‖ public_meta_hash ‖ declared_epoch(LE u64) ‖ joiner_agent_id ‖ invite_secret_hash ‖ admission_cert_digest)`.
   `roster_root_after_add` is computed by CLONING the authority's live `GroupInfo` (exactly as the production authority does before sealing — named_groups.rs:10513-10520 on base `3030de4`), applying the seat-write the authority is about to perform, and taking `compute_roster_root` over the result. It is therefore derived from the authority's ACTUAL CURRENT roster at mandate time — including every roster change that landed between invite mint and join — never from the possibly-stale invite projection (r2 P1: with the invite minted at N over `{owner,A}`, B admitted at N+1, the production terminal root covers `{owner,A,B,joiner}`; the v1 recipe would have covered only `{owner,A,joiner}` and could never verify). `parent_state_hash` is the authority's current head hash at the same clone point, so `expected_terminal_revision = current_revision + 1`. `declared_epoch` is the INTENDED TreeKEM epoch. RECEIVER RULE (on an ISOLATED CANDIDATE, never the live state): the enforcing member first applies the event to a throwaway clone of its `GroupInfo` — exactly the production apply pattern — recomputes `roster_root_after_add` from that ISOLATED post-apply candidate, and compares it to the mandate's value AND to the terminal commit's signed `roster_root` — all three must agree BEFORE any authoritative mutation or persistence; a failed mandate comparison leaves the member's state byte-identical (the candidate is discarded). Where the member's roster cannot re-derive the authority's current shape (itself behind), the mandate fails closed as `invite_base_inconsistent`-class on the candidate, again with no live-state change.
   The mandate still does NOT sign the terminal commit's signature, its TreeKEM-derived binding, or any post-mutation artifact: the actual terminal is bound by the EXISTING post-mutation head attestation, which CAS-checks `(attested head == terminal parent)` — and this design additionally requires the terminal's actual epoch to equal `declared_epoch` at attestation-verify time. Two-phase closure: mandate = pre-mutation intent over deterministic inputs; attestation = post-mutation confirmation; both must verify.
   *Rejected variant (r1):* deriving from the INVITE projection — proven unverifiable after any intervening roster change (the concrete `{owner,A,B,joiner}` sequence above). *Rejected variant (r0):* dropping the terminal binding from the mandate entirely (parent+roster only) and relying on the attestation alone — strictly weaker: a mandate could then be replayed against a different post-add roster shape that still chains from the same parent.

   **1b — Capability and grace state machine (exact; keyed per member, per group, PER AUTHORITY AGENT).** Production permits multiple active Admin-or-higher committers and `MemberAdded` authenticates the event actor as the sender, so a member can observe a valid mandate from admin A and an absent-mandate event from admin B — one group-level state cannot implement that. The state is therefore a map `authority_agent_id → state`:
   - **Capability predicate** (no new gossip surface): an authority AGENT is *mandate-capable* iff the member has observed, from THAT agent, EITHER a valid mandate on any event OR an owner-axis invite whose InviteV4 countersignature that agent's install produced (the countersignature proves the issuing install holds the owner USER key). The record is per-agent: A's capability never implicates B.
   - **States (per authority agent):** `Unknown` (never observed capability from this agent) → warn-accept that agent's absent mandates indefinitely — the keyless tier, NOT a grace case. `Capable{grace_started_at}` — recorded on this agent's first capability observation, persisted locally as `mandate_capability: BTreeMap<agent_id_hex, MandateCapabilityState>` (serde-default, outside the state hash, bootstrap-stripped). `Refusing` — after this agent's `grace_started_at + mandate_grace`; an absent-mandate owner-axis `MemberAdded` whose EVENT ACTOR is a `Refusing`-state agent is refused with the typed, retryable `owner_mandate_missing` (counted per-agent in diagnostics; no destructive state). An absent mandate from a DIFFERENT, still-`Unknown` admin is unaffected. Any subsequently observed VALID mandate from an agent moves that agent's entry back to `Capable` with the clock retained (no reset).
   - **Group-policy alternative, explicitly rejected:** collapsing to one group-level state (any-capable ⇒ all-refuse-after-grace) — it would refuse events from genuinely keyless secondary admins the moment one capable admin exists, breaking the shipped multi-admin model (`require_admin_or_above` admits every Admin-or-higher inviter); the per-agent map preserves it. Availability consequence of the chosen per-agent design: an attacker who IS a recorded-capable admin and deliberately omits mandates is refused; an attacker who is a never-observed admin omits freely (bounded by all other ADR-0016 checks); documenting that boundary is a human ratification item.
   - **Clock:** local persisted wall-clock (the same already-trusted source as invite expiry); window default 60 days (one release cycle), config override `mandate_grace_days`, documented in SKILL.md. No fleet clock, no consensus on the deadline — divergence is safe because both states fail closed on evidence.
   - **No mandate-capable authority reachable:** unchanged shipped semantics — join stays Tier-2 with head attestation + evidence + (this ADR) quarantine; refusal never triggers from mere unreachability, only from a recorded-capable authority omitting the mandate post-grace.
2. **Alternate-chain validation = ancestor-walk + owner head anchor; NO automated eviction.** Fork/eviction decisions validate the complete alternate chain from the common ancestor (committer active-admin at each predecessor), and for OwnerCertified groups additionally require an owner-signed anchor at the fork point. Where that anchor is unavailable (old-key attack), the outcome is quarantine on authenticated evidence, never a decision made with the contested key.
3. **Persistent PER-NODE quarantine, staged route coverage — containment scope is explicitly LOCAL, never fleet.** A persisted, stable-id-keyed quarantine marker fed by authenticated ForkEvidence. Scope statement: the marker, the forensic snapshot, and therefore the containment are properties of the OBSERVING node only; a member that never receives the authenticated ForkEvidence has no marker and is NOT contained — that is the defined, accepted scope, not an oversight. Fleet-wide containment is REJECTED for this ADR: propagating quarantine markers would need an authenticated propagation/acknowledgement channel whose replay/pollution surface is exactly what ADR-0059's dedup just closed, and no member can authenticate another member's local observation. Per-node clear semantics (r4 fix: the r3 CONVERGENCE QUORUM is REMOVED — two successive valid commits by colluding admins on the contested ancestry satisfy any such rule, and it contradicted the rejected Option 6 / no-quorum scope; retained ancestry alone is NOT proof of canonicity): the marker clears ONLY when this node applies a commit C such that (a) C's revision is STRICTLY GREATER than the evidenced fork revision (a same-revision sibling — the contested branch itself — can never clear), (b) C validates under ADR-0016 AND its `prev_state_hash` ancestry resolves through this node's RETAINED chain back to the fork revision, and (c) C carries a VALID OWNER ANCHOR (owner mandate or owner head attestation) — owner-axis groups ONLY. For NON-owner-axis groups there is NO independent canonical anchor in this design: quarantine is PRESERVED indefinitely once set (surfaced as `quarantine_no_anchor`), and RECOVERY IS EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED — a manual operator procedure (reconcile the branches out-of-band, re-issue a fresh signed chain, or migrate members to a new group) is the only path, deliberately not automated here; inventing an automatic clear would recreate exactly the undecidable-sibling eviction this ADR rejects. Negative control (validation requirement): a fixture where the contested branch itself publishes a valid higher-revision commit chaining from the CONTESTED ancestry must NOT clear the marker; only an owner-anchored canonical-ancestry commit does, and for non-owner-axis groups nothing does automatically. Route coverage in this ADR: the membership-gated group surfaces (public send, secure encrypt/open) — routes that already fail closed on membership. History/delegations/kv and the ratchet lifecycle epoch token are explicitly enumerated as the follow-on review, not silently included.
4. **Forensic fork snapshot.** On first-complete evidence, persist a redacted snapshot (both conflicting commit headers, roster roots, policy/meta hashes; `shared_secret` and KEM material excluded) alongside the quarantine marker for post-hoc authority review.

## Migration / Compatibility

| Population | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Mandate-aware authority + old members | Old members ignore the unknown `owner_mandate` field (serde default) — no wire break; they keep ADR-0016 validation. |
| Old authority + mandate-aware members | `Unknown` capability state ⇒ warn-accept indefinitely (Decision §1b); refusal begins only after the member RECORDS capability (valid mandate or countersigned invite observed) and the 60-day local grace elapses — the deadline is per-node persisted, never a fleet clock. |
| Keyless-owner v0.41 admins | Cannot mint mandates or countersign invites (same `owner_key_unavailable` fence); members that never observe capability stay `Unknown` ⇒ warn-accept indefinitely — the keyless tier, distinct from grace. |
| Non-owner-axis groups | Unchanged: ADR-0016 chain validation + evidence + quarantine (the quarantine gate is policy-agnostic; eviction remains out of scope). |
| Bootstrap/signed-public snapshots | Quarantine marker and forensic snapshot are local-only: stripped outbound, rejected inbound, exactly like `invite_lineage` (same sanitizer + validator sites). |
| Upgrade order | Authorities/owners first (mandate production), then members (enforcement), then the grace window closes; joins during the window rely on InviteV4 + head attestation, which already shipped. |

## Attack / Regression matrix

| Scenario | v0.41.x (today) | This design |
|---|---|---|
| #468 stale-base fork to a joiner | Tier-2 adoption + evidence | Adoption now requires the mandate (owner axis) or stays Tier-2; the OBSERVING joiner's local data-plane is contained (per-node scope; fleet containment is out of scope and rejected) |
| Forged MemberAdded by a non-blessed admin (gapless) | Applies on all members (G1) | Refused by mandate validation on every enforcing member |
| Removed-admin fork eviction attempt | Possible with retained-base validation (G2) | Ancestor-walk rejects; no owner anchor ⇒ quarantine, never eviction |
| Promoted-after-base admin chain | Wrongly refused (G2) | Accepted: predecessor-roster walk sees the promotion |
| Equal-revision valid-sibling forks | Undecidable (ADR-0016 D7) | Owner-axis: owner anchor decides on the next owner-anchored advance; non-owner-axis: preserved quarantine, manual recovery (undecided by design) |
| Evidence replay/pollution | Deduped (ADR-0059) | Same dedupe feeds the persistent marker; marker clears only on owner-anchored advance |
| Restart mid-quarantine | Transient flag lost | Persisted marker survives; forensic snapshot survives |
| Mixed fleet join during migration | Works (InviteV4) | Unchanged during grace; refusal only after grace with mandate-capable authority |
| Regression risk: false quarantine | n/a | Bounded by authenticated-evidence-only trigger + owner-anchored clear + explicit unquarantine runbook in ops docs |

## Consequences

- Positive: the invite-derived trust root is owner-anchored end-to-end for OwnerCertified groups; fork conflicts become operationally visible and contained without risking split-brain eviction; the residual from ADR-0016 §7/ADR-0059 shrinks to "equal-revision siblings without an owner anchor", which is documented rather than hidden.
- Negative/cost: one more owner-key dependency at the authority (keyless admins degrade a tier); the grace window temporarily weakens enforcement to warning-level; quarantine false-positives (mitigated: authenticated-evidence-only, owner-anchored clear); route coverage is staged, so history/delegations remain un-quarantined until the follow-on ADR.
- Explicitly NOT decided here: automated eviction (rejected), quorum anchors (rejected — including the r3 convergence-quorum variant, removed), NON-OWNER-AXIS quarantine recovery (unresolved: preserved indefinitely, manual operator procedure documented, no automatic clear), the ratchet lifecycle epoch token design, kv/history/delegation route gating, and the #492/#481/#372/#471 repairs (owned elsewhere).

## Validation

- Design-level (this ADR): source-triage pinning of every gap with file:line on base `3030de4` (`issue-472-source-triage.md` in the campaign review artifacts, base corrected per independent review), the rejected-alternatives record above, the migration matrix, and the round-2 fixes: deterministic pre-mutation mandate preimage (§1a), the capability/grace state machine (§1b), per-node quarantine scope (§3), and the corrected source base.
- Implementation (future PRs, each with its own gates): unit tests for mandate sign/verify/tamper per surface; ancestor-walk promoted-admin and removed-admin fixtures; quarantine persistence across restart + bootstrap strip/reject; negative controls (no quarantine without authenticated evidence; the marker CANNOT be cleared by the contested branch's own valid commits — strict-revision + retained-ancestry + OWNER ANCHOR ONLY (the r3 convergence quorum is removed; non-owner-axis groups never auto-clear), with an explicit contested-branch-cannot-clear fixture; mandate mismatch after an intervening roster change — the r2 `{owner,A,B,joiner}` sequence — verifies under the current-roster derivation and fails under the rejected invite-projection variant); mixed-version wire fixtures both directions; the four ordered Rust gates on the final tree.
- The minimal release-safe guard (persistent `fork_quarantined` bit consulted by membership-gated routes only) is separable and may land first; see the triage artifact's Guard A analysis.
